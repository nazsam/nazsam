Incident Response

What are the NIST 800-61 incident response phases?

Answer

Preparation → Detection & Analysis → Containment → Eradication → Recovery → Post-Incident Activity. Loop back to improve playbooks after each incident.

What is the first action when ransomware is detected on a workstation?

Answer

Isolate the host from the network (disable Wi-Fi/Ethernet, VLAN quarantine) while preserving memory/disk for forensics — avoid rebooting or paying before assessment.

Containment vs eradication — explain the difference.

Answer

Containment stops spread (block C2, disable account, isolate segment). Eradication removes attacker presence (patch vuln, remove malware, rotate creds, close persistence).

What is a tabletop exercise?

Answer

Discussion-based simulation of an incident scenario — tests roles, communications, and decision-making without touching production systems. Low cost, high value for IR readiness.

What information belongs in an initial incident ticket?

Answer

Detection source, affected assets/users, timeline, severity hypothesis, assigned commander, containment status, and links to evidence — update as facts change.

When should you involve legal and PR in an incident?

Answer

When PII/PHI breach, regulatory notification thresholds, media exposure, or law enforcement coordination is likely — early engagement avoids costly mistakes.

What is chain of custody in forensics?

Answer

Documented handling of evidence — who collected, when, storage, hash values — so it remains admissible and untampered for investigation or legal proceedings.

How do you prioritize incidents during a widespread campaign?

Answer

By business impact, confirmed compromise vs suspicious, crown-jewel assets, active exfiltration, and exploitability — use a severity matrix, not FIFO.

What is a lessons-learned report?

Answer

Post-incident document: timeline, root cause, what worked/failed, action items with owners — feeds back into detection, training, and architecture fixes.

Why rotate credentials after containment?

Answer

Attackers may have extracted tokens/passwords during dwell time. Rotation closes persistent access even after malware removal.
